# Product Blueprint — AcquireOS AI
## Mission
Make accountable customer acquisition available to solo businesses, agencies and enterprises through an AI Growth Manager.
## Jobs to be done
Understand my company; recommend acquisition strategy; make compliant creatives; launch approved campaigns; qualify inbound leads; follow up with consent; show which spend generated appointments, purchases and revenue.
## Personas
Owner/operator, growth manager, agency operator, sales representative, enterprise administrator, compliance reviewer and platform operator.
## Core workflow
1. Create tenant and invite teammates.
2. Enter website/catalogue, region, business category, offer and target KPI.
3. Connect Meta account and WhatsApp Business through OAuth.
4. Review evidence-backed AI strategy and forecast ranges.
5. Approve ad copy, creatives, target audiences, daily and lifetime spend caps.
6. Publish via channel API, record external IDs and approval.
7. Attribute leads and conversions; route qualified leads to team.
8. Recommend tests and optimizations; execute only authorized actions.
## MVP
Web SaaS, organization RBAC, catalog upload, AI strategy, human-reviewed creative, Meta advertising connection, basic performance ingestion, first-party landing forms, lead CRM, opt-in WhatsApp templates, outcome dashboard, subscription billing, audit events.
## Non-goals for MVP
Cross-platform autonomous spend, proprietary ad network, unsolicited outreach scraping, unapproved customer messaging, guarantees of ROI.
## Monetization
Platform subscription + metered AI usage and optional managed-service fees; advertising charges paid directly to ad networks. No claims of pure profit or guaranteed results.
## Success
Activation, time to first approved campaign, connected-account success, qualified lead rate, cost per qualified lead, booked appointments, conversion/retention and customer-attributed margin. Measure with cohorts, attribution windows and confidence labels.
## Launch market
Start with Ghanaian SMEs and agencies, with infrastructure extensible globally. Ghana Data Protection Act and marketing/telecom obligations require legal review before launch.

# Privacy and Data Governance
Define roles of controller/processor for each flow. Record legal basis, collection purpose, retention and recipients per data category; avoid collecting sensitive attributes without clear lawful necessity. Provide DSAR export, correction and deletion workflows, subject to legal record retention. Pseudonymize analytics. Keep marketing consent receipts with source, purpose, timestamp and revocation. Honor suppression lists. Keep tenant data segregated, support customer data export and documented vendor subprocessors and transfer safeguards. Prohibit using one tenant's identifiable content for another tenant's AI strategy without explicit permission. Review Ghana and international laws with counsel before launch.

# Compliance and Responsible Advertising
Follow applicable advertising standards, platform-specific restricted industry rules, anti-spam/telecom rules, privacy laws, consumer protection and truthful substantiation of claims. Build an approval gate for performance promises, regulated services, targeted audiences, rights to media and testimonials. Store evidence for claims, licenses and use permissions. Do not fabricate testimonials, case studies, performance statistics, customer photos or guaranteed outcomes. Geography matters: local counsel must review Ghana, EU/UK and other expansion requirements. This document is engineering guidance, not legal advice.

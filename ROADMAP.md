# Delivery Roadmap
## Phase 0: Foundation
Architecture decisions, scope, design system, threat model, customer interviews, provider sandboxes, contracts and DPIA.
## Phase 1: Secure platform
Org registration, RBAC, RLS, MFA/admin controls, brand profiles, assets, billing scaffolding, audit trails, observability.
## Phase 2: Strategy and creative
AI Growth Manager, structured briefs, strategy proposals, creative drafts, claim substantiation, previews, approval workflow.
## Phase 3: Campaign execution
Meta OAuth, ad-account inventory, supported campaign publishing, readback reconciliation, budgets, webhooks, safe rollback.
## Phase 4: Outcomes
Forms, CRM, consent capture, WhatsApp opt-in, lead qualification, sales handoff, attribution and executive dashboard.
## Phase 5: Optimization
Experiments, holdouts, supervised recommendations, bounded automation and performance monitoring.
## Phase 6: Expansion
Google, TikTok, LinkedIn, e-commerce integrations, agency workspaces, enterprise SSO, regional compliance.
## Exit gates
Each phase requires automated tests, access-control test suite, abuse/security review, rollback, documentation and product acceptance criteria. No unsupervised financial actions in initial phases.
